title: The hand, and the flock
---

The hero now has a hand made of particles beside the recording, posed through the same gestures the recording was taken from, in time with it. Away from the hero the particles fly as one flock. Scrolling back up re-forms the hero: the line's dots come round a drawn route and pen the line from left to right, and the hand's dots gather into a spinning funnel and build the hand from the wrist up. The demo's map turned 3D, using the classifier's third discriminant axis, and slowly swings so the clouds read as a solid. A slow GPU now keeps the particles at a lower resolution instead of switching them off.
