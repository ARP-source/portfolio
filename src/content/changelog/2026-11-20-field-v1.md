title: Particle field, first version
---

A GPU particle field behind the page, loaded after the page has painted. In the hero the particles sit on the same real GRABMyo recording the static trace draws, and scrolling moves through it. Elsewhere they drift and move aside for the pointer. Phones get fewer particles, slow or software-only GPUs keep the static trace, and reduced motion gets a still frame. The page reads the same with JavaScript off.
