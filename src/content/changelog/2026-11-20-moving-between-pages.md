title: Moving between pages
---

Some of the flock now draws the graph of the project card you're looking at, from left to right. Opening a project carries those dots into the graph on its page while the title glides up to the top. Once the particles are running, pages change in place. The next page is fetched while the pointer rests on its link, and the particles keep moving through the change instead of starting over.
